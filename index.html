DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

Operating model

Designed as an operational homelab, not a simulated cloud dashboard. The architecture emphasizes reproducibility, recoverability and identity integration within limited hardware resources.

$ aquavault architecture runtime: raspberry-pi-500-plus orchestrator: k3s nodes: 1 delivery: argocd identity: authentik applications: workos, nextcloud recovery: encrypted-image-vault availability: single-node
